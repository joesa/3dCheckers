    How to Play

    Getting Started

    • Pass & Play — duel a friend on this device; turns alternate between Ember and Frost.
    • Play vs Storm — solo duel against the machine at four tiers: Squire (forgiving), Knight, Warlock, and Storm Monarch (brutal). You always lead as Ember.
    • Online Duel — tap Create Invite, send the code to your friend (any chat app works). They paste it, send their answer back, you paste that and tap Connect. No accounts, no servers — the two browsers talk directly.
    • Same-Browser Tabs — create a room code, open this page in a second tab and join with the same code.

    Controls

    • Click one of your pieces — glowing tiles show its legal moves (green = move, red = capture). Click a tile to go.
    • Drag to orbit the island · scroll to zoom · click empty ground to deselect.
    • Ember moves first. The avatars' hands fly in whenever a piece is played — yours and theirs.

    Rules — English Draughts

    • Men move one step diagonally forward onto an empty dark square.
    • Capture by leaping over an adjacent enemy into the empty square beyond; the enemy is removed.
    • Jumps are forced. If a capture exists you must take one — and multi-jump chains must be completed in one move.
    • Reach the far rank to crown a piece. Kings move and capture in all four diagonals; a piece crowned mid-chain ends its turn at once.
    • Win by capturing every enemy piece, or by leaving your opponent with no legal move.
    • Online duels run a 45s turn timer — on timeout a legal move plays itself.
